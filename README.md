# Nflorest-Architecture-Design

An agent cannot reliably execute a business objective unless the system provides the objective, relevant knowledge, constraints, tools and success criteria.
Agent instructions: Tell an agent how to perform its task.
Business configuration: Tell the system what the business wants and permits.
Runtime data: The actual businesses, contacts and research the system discovers.

ICP dimension	What it tells the system	Example
Business category	What type of business to find	Florist
Business model	Which type of florist	Independent florist
Geography	Where to find them	United Kingdom
Business activity	Whether they are operational	Actively selling flowers
Online presence	Relevant digital presence	Operating website
Business characteristics	What makes them attractive	Relevant products, online ordering
Exclusions	Which businesses to avoid	Wholesalers, artificial flower-only businesses

What must we provide beyond the ICP?
You should think in terms of separate business configurations.
1. ICP — WHO

What type of business is a potential customer?
2. Market configuration — WHERE
Which countries, regions, cities and market segments should be targeted?
3. Source policy — WHERE CAN WE LOOK
Which directories, websites, databases and other sources are approved?
4. Discovery requirements — WHAT INFORMATION DO WE NEED
Which business details must be collected, and what evidence must be recorded?
5. Qualification policy — WHICH BUSINESSES ARE GOOD ENOUGH
Which criteria are mandatory, which are preferences, and how is a decision made?
6. Contact policy — WHOM MAY WE CONTACT
Which contact roles and email types are appropriate and legally eligible?
7. Messaging policy — WHAT SHOULD WE SAY
What is the approved value proposition, tone, personalization approach and prohibited content?
8. Suppression data — WHO MUST BE EXCLUDED
Existing customers, previous opt-outs, do-not-contact records and other exclusions.
9. Execution policy — HOW MUCH AND WHEN
Daily email targets, prospect acquisition targets, campaign limits, schedules, budgets and safety controls
